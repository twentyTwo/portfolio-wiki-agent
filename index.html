Portfolio Wiki Agent

A personal assistant tool built by Noor Alam Shuvo for his own use. It creates and manages his to-do items and reminders in his own Google Tasks list, driven from a private notes repository.

  • Single user: the app is used only by its developer.
  • Uses one Google permission: Google Tasks (read and write the developer's own tasks).
  • Not a public service and not offered to other users.
